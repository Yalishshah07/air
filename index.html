Flagship Academic & Research Headquarters

At the Foothills of the Margalla Hills

Established under a Federal Charter in 2002 under the patronage of the Pakistan Air Force, the Sector E-9 Main Campus is the intellectual core of Air University. Its purpose-built academic blocks, specialized engineering laboratories, and serene green courtyards foster focused scholarship and interdisciplinary discovery.

The campus houses nationally recognized research centers—including the National Center for Cyber Security (NCCS) and dedicated Artificial Intelligence and Robotics labs—alongside the Central Digital Library providing round-the-clock access to international IEEE, ACM, and Springer research repositories.

National Accreditations

Fully accredited by HEC, Pakistan Engineering Council (PEC), NCEAC for computing, and NBEAC for business education.

Campus Environment

Purpose-built auditoriums, sports facilities, student innovation societies, and direct industry linkages in Islamabad.
